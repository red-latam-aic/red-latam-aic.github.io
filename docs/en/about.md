# About us
We are Latin American scientists located in various parts of the world (Europe, North America, and South America).

We work on different topics related to AI applied to science.

You can find a list of current members [here]({{'/en/us/' | relative_url }})


## Mission

Our mission is to cluster, support, and foster dynamic collaborations among Latin American scientists in the field of artificial intelligence. This effort aims to create spaces that facilitate such partnerships for the current and future generations of Latin American researchers.

## Vision

Become a bridge between different regions of Latin America and the world, connecting students with opportunities and facilitating interactions between the public and private sectors in applications of artificial intelligence in the sciences.

## Objectives

1. Build an interactive directory with up-to-date information on Latin American researchers working in artificial intelligence, both within and outside Latin America.
2. Promote workshops, talks, conferences, and dialogues within and beyond the scientific community.
3. Connect students and researchers within and outside Latin America by organizing mentorship programs.
4. Maintain a repository with up-to-date links and information in Spanish, Portuguese, and French on new applications of artificial intelligence in the sciences.
5. Provide resources and online courses of interest to the community.
6. Foster connections between the academic, governmental, and technology sectors across Latin America.
